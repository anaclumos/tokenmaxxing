title: Iyakoki na gaskiya
description: "Ciniki-cinikin da ke zuwa da hot-swap na haɗaɗɗun asusu"
---

- **Zagaye sanyi guda.** Ajiyar prompt tana iyakance ga ƙungiya, don haka zagaye na farko a kan sabon asusu yana sake loda mahalli sau ɗaya.
- **Tsangwamar dakatarwar-ƙarewa.** Kawai tafki da ya ƙare gaba ɗaya ne ke tsayar da zaman domin ƙirgar baya; duk abin da aka rubuta a wannan ɗan lokaci yana ɓacewa.
- **Jinkirin amincewa.** A kan macOS zagaye na farko cikin kusan daƙiƙa 30 na musanya na iya har yanzu auna tsohon asusu; sararin sandunan yana sha shi.
- **Girman fashewa da ake rabawa.** Dukkan zaman bayanan martaba na tsohuwa suna raba tantancewa guda da ke aiki, don haka musanya tana motsa su duka tare (kowanne yana amincewa a wurinsa).
- **Tantancewa bayyananne a Linux.** Claude Code da kanta tana adana tantancewar Linux a matsayin fayil ɗin rubutu bayyananne na 0600; kwafin da tokenmaxxing ke ajiye suna bin model iri ɗaya.
