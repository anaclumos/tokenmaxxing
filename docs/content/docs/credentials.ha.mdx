---
title: Ajiyar tantancewa da tsarin aminci
description: "Inda tantancewa suke a kowane dandali da dokokin da kowace musanya ke tilastawa"
---

Dukkan yanayi yana zaune a `~/.config/tokenmaxxing/`. I/O na tantancewa yana bi ta wani facade na ma'aji guda da aka zaɓa bisa dandali; wuraren kira ba sa taɓa reshe bisa dandali.

**macOS**: tantancewa suna zaune cikin keychain na shiga a matsayin abubuwan generic-password, daidai da inda Claude Code da kanta ke ajiye su. Abin da ke aiki shi ne wanda claude ke karantawa; ajiyar kowane asusu da ke cikin tafki ana ajiye ta a matsayin abin keychain nata na `tokenmaxxing-cred-<uuid8>`. Babu wani abin da ke a matsayin rubutu bayyananne a diski. Karatu yana amfani da `security find-generic-password -w` (sirrin yana bayyana ne kawai a stdout); rubutu yana tura layin `add-generic-password` cikin `security -i` ta stdin don sirrin kada ya taɓa bayyana a argv na wani tsari. Yanayin hulɗa yana da buffer na layi kusan 4 KB, don haka blobs masu girman gaske suna komawa zuwa rubutun argv - dalili guda da ya sa abubuwan da aka ajiye ke adana kawai ƙaramin abin OAuth.

**Linux**: Claude Code yana adana tantancewa a matsayin fayil ɗin rubutu bayyananne na 0600, kuma tokenmaxxing yana bin model iri ɗaya: fayil ɗin da ke aiki shi ne `.credentials.json` na claude, kuma ajiyoyin da aka ajiye fayilolin atomic ne na 0600 ƙarƙashin `~/.config/tokenmaxxing/creds/` cikin babban shirin 0700. Atomic a nan yana nufin rubuta-zuwa-na-ɗan-lokaci, fsync, sannan a sake suna: mai karatu a lokaci guda koyaushe yana ganin cikakken tsohon fayil ko cikakken sabon fayil, ba wani ɓangare ba.

Dokokin aminci da musanya ke tilastawa:

- **Tattarawa ta ainihin mai shi.** Kafin musanya, ana warware ainihin mai tantancewar da ke aiki ta hanyar tambayar API wane asusu ne token ɗin ke ciki (`fetchTokenIdentity` a kan OAuth profile endpoint, ana daidaita ta da UUID na asusun) - ba ta hanyar ƙungiya ba, domin kowane memba a cikin shirin Team yana raba ƙungiya guda, kuma ba ta hanyar dogaro da lakabin mai aiki da aka ajiye ba, wanda zai iya karkata. Lakabin da ya karkata ya taɓa sa tattarawar asusu ɗaya ta rubuta kan ajiyar kaɗai ta wani asusu. Idan tantancewar da ke aiki ta wani asusu ne a wajen tafki, musanya tana ƙi kai tsaye maimakon ta lalata ta.
- **Sashe guda mai muhimmanci.** Tattarawa, shigar da sabuwar tantancewa, ajiyar token ɗinta da aka juya, musanyar asusu na `~/.claude.json`, da tabbatar da lakabin mai aiki duk suna faruwa a cikin sashe guda da aka kulle.
- **Haɗuwa da sabuntawa na claude.** Zaman da ke kusa da ƙarewa na iya juya token ɗinsa a kowane lokaci, don haka rubuce-rubucen tantancewa suna kuma ɗaukar makullin sabuntawa na Claude Code (mafi ƙoƙari, tare da dawo da tsohon makulli da iyakar lokaci mai iyaka).
- **Grants matattu suna raguwa cikin tsafta.** Sabuntawa da ta dawo `invalid_grant` tana yiwa wancan asusu alama cewa yana buƙatar sake-tantancewa kuma tana adana alamar kafin kuskuren ya yaɗu, don haka madaukan sake-gwadawa suna rage rukunin 'yan takara kuma su ƙare. Tantancewar da Claude Code da kanta ta share bayan sabuntawa da ya gaza (tokens na access da na sabuntawa da suke fanko) ana gane ta a matsayin matacciya ba tare da kashe buƙata ba. Kafin a kashe token na sabuntawa da aka ajiye, ana warware mai shi ta hanyar profile endpoint; tantancewar da aka ajiye wadda ta warware zuwa wani asusu daban ana yiwa ta alama maimakon a sabunta ta, domin sabunta kwafin grant mai aiki na wani asusu yana soke zaman-zaman wancan asusu. Duk wani ƙin yarda daga endpoint na token (wani HTTP 4xx da ba `invalid_grant` ba) yana ware wannan ɗan takara daga sauran kimantawar kuma baya yiwa komai alama. `tokenmaxxing auth` yana gyara asusun da aka yiwa alama a wurinsa, kuma yana ƙin shigar da ta sauka a kan asusu daban da wanda ake gyara.
- **Babu rubuta token.** Tokens ba sa taɓa bayyana a logs, kurakurai, ko fitarwa. Jikin kuskure daga endpoints na token da amfani ba a taɓa fitar da su danye; kawai rukunin filaye da aka amince da su ne ke tsira, domin jikin gazawar endpoint na token na iya sake maimaita kayan buƙata.
- **Tsabtar probe.** Duk probe na `claude` da aka ƙirƙira yana share masu canjin muhalli guda takwas da claude ke girmamawa kafin binciken keychain ɗinsa (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN`, `CLAUDE_SECURESTORAGE_CONFIG_DIR`, da sauransu), don kada a taɓa danganta probe ga asusu da ba daidai ba.
