title: "Gadar Slack: xx serve"
description: "xx serve yana gudanar da zaman Claude Code daga zaruruwan Slack ta Socket Mode"
---

`tokenmaxxing serve` yana gudanar da daemon na Slack na gida ta Socket Mode - babu buƙatar URL na jama'a. Saitawa:

```sh
tokenmaxxing serve setup                    # yana buga manifest na aikace-aikacen, yana neman tokens biyu
tokenmaxxing serve link <channel-id> <repo> # haɗa tashar zuwa checkout na repo
tokenmaxxing serve                          # gudanar da daemon
```

Ambaci bot ɗin a cikin tashar da aka haɗa kuma zai buɗe zaman Claude Code na wancan zare, a cikin checkout na repo da aka haɗa. Kowane saƙon zare yana gudanar da `query()` guda na Agent SDK tare da `resume` - ba tsayayyen tambaya mai gudana ba - don haka `ensureBestAccount()` yana sake auna tafkin a kowane zagaye ɗaya kuma daemon zai iya sake farawa ba tare da rasa zaruruwa ba (bayanan zare suna dawwama; daemon yana sake biyan kuɗi (re-subscribe) su lokacin farawa).

Halayen da suka cancanci lura:

- **Kwarara (Streaming)**: amsoshi suna kwarara a asali cikin Slack, tare da katunan aiki don tunani da gudanar da kayan aiki, an nuna lambar da aka kewaye tare da haskakawa, da katin "Turn" na rufewa tare da farashi da tsawon lokaci; duk katunan zagaye suna naɗewa cikin block na shiri (plan block) guda mai iya faɗaɗawa, don zagaye yawanci ya karanta a matsayin saƙo guda: amsar da kuma dropdown guda na ayyukansa (sake-gwadawa a tsakiyar zagaye ko ƙarawar da aka ƙi tana buɗe saƙo mai biyowa). Kiran TodoWrite yana sabunta katin lissafin dubawa guda mai tsayayye a wurinsa.
- **Izini**: `permissionMode` na kowace haɗi tsohuwa ita ce `acceptEdits`; `--yolo` (laƙabi `--dangerous`) yana sa haɗi ta shiga `bypassPermissions`. Ba a yarda da tattaunawa ta hulɗa a cikin zagayen da aka isar ba - babu wanda zai iya amsa ɗaya ta Slack - don haka model yana tambaya cikin rubutu kuma amsarka ta zare ita ce zagaye na gaba. Wani skill na plugin da aka haɗa yana koya masa ya yiwa mai neman alama kuma ya ƙare zagaye lokacin da yake buƙatar shawara.
- **Umarnin slash**: saƙon zare wanda rubutunsa da aka cire ya fara da `/` yana gudana a matsayin umarnin slash na claude. Mawallafin Slack yana cin saƙonni da suka fara da `/`, don haka yi amfani da sigar ambato-farko (`@bot /usage`) ko sarari na farko.
- **Rufewa cikin annashuwa**: SIGTERM/SIGINT suna zubarwa - ana jefar da sabbin zagaye da ƙarfi, zagayen da ke tafiya suna samun har zuwa mintuna 5, kuma yaron claude yana gudana a ware cikin ƙungiyar tsarinsa don Ctrl-C na tashar kada ya iya kashe shi kafin zubarwa ta kiyaye zagaye.
- **Tsaftar zare**: lokacin da ka gaya masa cewa an gama aikin, model yana kiran kayan aikin `finish_thread` na cikin-tsari; bayan zagaye ya ƙare gaba ɗaya, daemon yana jefar da bayanan zare, yana dakatar da biyan kuɗi (unsubscribe), kuma yana buga tabbaci guda. Sabon ambato yana fara sabon zama.
- **Tsabtar kuskure**: kurakuran zagaye suna buga rubutun-saƙo-kawai da aka gyara, ba jikin kuskure danye ba.
