title: "Gadar Slack: xx serve"
description: "xx serve yana gudanar da zaman Claude Code daga zaruruwan Slack ta Socket Mode"
---

`tokenmaxxing serve` yana gudanar da daemon na Slack na gida ta Socket Mode - babu buƙatar URL na jama'a. Saitawa:

```sh
tokenmaxxing serve setup                    # yana buga manifest na aikace-aikacen, yana neman tokens biyu
tokenmaxxing serve link <channel-id> <repo> # haɗa tashar zuwa checkout na repo
tokenmaxxing serve                          # gudanar da daemon
```

Ambaci bot ɗin a cikin tashar da aka haɗa kuma zai buɗe zaman Claude Code na wancan zare, a cikin checkout na repo da aka haɗa. Kowane sabon zagaye yana gudanar da `query()` guda na Agent SDK tare da `resume` - don haka `ensureBestAccount()` yana sake auna tafkin a kowane zagaye kuma daemon zai iya sake farawa ba tare da rasa zaruruwa ba (bayanan zare suna dawwama; daemon yana sake biyan kuɗi (re-subscribe) su lokacin farawa) - amma amsar da ta iso yayin da wata tambaya ke ci gaba da gudana take shiga cikin shigarwar mai kwarara ta wannan tambayar, maimakon ta gudanar da tambayarta; amsar da ta rasa taga jagorantarwa tana gudana a matsayin zagaye na gaba.

Halayen da suka cancanci lura:

- **Jagorantarwa (Steering)**: idan amsa ta iso yayin da zagayen zaren ke ci gaba da gudana, tana naɗewa cikin wannan zagayen - irin allurar tsaka-tsakin-zagaye da Claude Code ke amfani da ita ta hulɗa kai tsaye - don haka model yana ganin ta a iyakar kayan aiki na gaba kuma yana daidaita hanya maimakon ya gama abin da ba daidai ba tukuna. Amsar da ta iso bayan kiran kayan aiki na ƙarshe na zagayen tana gudana a matsayin zagayenta na daban nan take bayan haka; babu abin da ake jefarwa a ko wace hanya. Saƙonnin da aka jagoranta suna ɗauke da hourglass reaction kuma suna zaunawa tare da zagayen, kuma rubutunsu yana hawa kan alama mai dawwama ta zagayen, don haka sake-farawa da ci gaba bayan iyakar amfani suna sake kunna shi. Sai saƙon da ke jira a bayan wani saƙo mai jira ne kaɗai ke tsallake naɗewar (ana kiyaye tsari), kuma daemon mai zubarwa har yanzu yana yin jefarwa da ƙarfi maimakon jagorantarwa.
- **Kwarara (Streaming)**: amsoshi suna kwarara a asali cikin Slack, tare da katunan aiki don tunani da gudanar da kayan aiki, an nuna lambar da aka kewaye tare da haskakawa, da katin "Turn" na rufewa tare da farashi da tsawon lokaci; duk katunan zagaye suna naɗewa cikin block na shiri (plan block) guda mai iya faɗaɗawa, don zagaye yawanci ya karanta a matsayin saƙo guda: amsar da kuma dropdown guda na ayyukansa (sake-gwadawa a tsakiyar zagaye ko ƙarawar da aka ƙi tana buɗe saƙo mai biyowa). Kiran TodoWrite yana sabunta katin lissafin dubawa guda mai tsayayye a wurinsa.
- **Izini**: `permissionMode` na kowace haɗi tsohuwa ita ce `acceptEdits`; `--yolo` (laƙabi `--dangerous`) yana sa haɗi ta shiga `bypassPermissions`. Ba a yarda da tattaunawa ta hulɗa a cikin zagayen da aka isar ba - babu wanda zai iya amsa ɗaya ta Slack - don haka model yana tambaya cikin rubutu kuma amsarka ta zare ita ce zagaye na gaba. Wani skill na plugin da aka haɗa yana koya masa ya yiwa mai neman alama kuma ya ƙare zagaye lokacin da yake buƙatar shawara.
- **Alamun matsayi (Status reactions)**: saƙon da ya jawo zagaye yana ɗauke da matsayinsa a matsayin emoji reaction: hourglass yayin da zagaye ke gudana, sannan alamar check (an gama), x (ya gaza), ko alamar tambaya (model yana buƙatar shawararka). Yana buƙatar izinin `reactions:write` daga manifest na yanzu; aikace-aikacen da suke akwai dole ne su sake liƙa manifest ɗin kuma su sake shigarwa.
- **Kula da tunatarwa (Attention and nudges)**: lokacin da model ya tambaye ka shawara yana kiran kayan aikin `need_attention` na cikin-tsari, don haka daemon ya san cewa zaren yana jiranka. Idan ka yi shiru na mintuna 10 zai yi maka alama sau ɗaya a cikin zaren. Yayin da zare ke jira, yin reaction ga tambayar ana isar da shi a matsayin amsarka (thumbs up yana zaɓar zaɓin da aka ba da shawara); duk wani reaction da ka ƙara a cikin zaren da ake bibiya ana naɗe shi cikin zagaye na gaba a matsayin mahallin bayani. Yana buƙatar `reactions:read` da abin da ya faru na `reaction_added`.
- **Umarnin slash**: saƙon zare wanda rubutunsa da aka cire ya fara da `/` yana gudana a matsayin umarnin slash na claude. Mawallafin Slack yana cin saƙonni da suka fara da `/`, don haka yi amfani da sigar ambato-farko (`@bot /usage`) ko sarari na farko.
- **Ci gaba ta atomatik bayan iyakar amfani**: idan kowane asusu da ke cikin tafki ya bugi iyakarsa, katsewa gajera tana ajiye saƙon sannan ta sake gwada shi a wurin; katsewa mai tsawo tana buga sanarwar jira sannan ta ci gaba da zagaye ta atomatik da zarar tafkin ya dawo, tana tsira daga sake-farawar daemon. Sai dai iyakar da ba a san lokacin dawowarta ba ce ke tambayarka ka sake tura saƙon.
- **Rufewa cikin annashuwa**: SIGTERM/SIGINT suna zubarwa - ana jefar da sabbin zagaye da ƙarfi, zagayen da ke tafiya suna samun har zuwa mintuna 5, kuma yaron claude yana gudana a ware cikin ƙungiyar tsarinsa don Ctrl-C na tashar kada ya iya kashe shi kafin zubarwa ta kiyaye zagaye.
- **Tsaftar zare**: lokacin da ka gaya masa cewa an gama aikin, model yana kiran kayan aikin `finish_thread` na cikin-tsari; bayan zagaye ya ƙare gaba ɗaya, daemon yana jefar da bayanan zare, yana dakatar da biyan kuɗi (unsubscribe), kuma yana buga tabbaci guda. Sabon ambato yana fara sabon zama.
- **Tsabtar kuskure**: kurakuran zagaye suna buga rubutun-saƙo-kawai da aka gyara, ba jikin kuskure danye ba.
