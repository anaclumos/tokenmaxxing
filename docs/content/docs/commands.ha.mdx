---
title: Umarni
description: "Kowane umarni na tokenmaxxing da abin da yake yi"
---

| Umarni | Abin da yake yi |
|---|---|
| `tokenmaxxing init` | Shigo da asusun yanzu, shigar da mai kula, hooks, da statusline |
| `tokenmaxxing init --codex` | Haka ga Codex: shigo da shiga, shigar da mai kula na codex da Stop hook |
| `tokenmaxxing add` | Yi rajistar ƙarin asusu (shiga a ware, an tattara shi cikin tafki) |
| `tokenmaxxing add --codex` | Yi rajistar ƙarin asusun Codex (shiga a ware) |
| `tokenmaxxing auth [sel \| --all]` | Sake tantance asusun da ke cikin tafki a wurinsa. Shi kaɗai yana lissafa tafkin kuma yana tambaya wanne; mai zaɓe yana nufin asusu ɗaya kuma yana gaya maka wace imel za ka shiga da ita; `--all` yana bin kowane asusun da ke buƙatar sake-tantancewa ɗaya bayan ɗaya |
| `tokenmaxxing switch [sel]` | Musanya zuwa asusu mafi kyau (greedy, idempotent) ko tilasta wani takamaimai |
| `tokenmaxxing switch --codex [sel]` | Musanya tafkin Codex (yana aiki a farawar codex ta gaba) |
| `tokenmaxxing ls` | Lissafa asusun da ke cikin tafki |
| `tokenmaxxing status` | Asusu tare da sandunan amfani na awa 5 / mako / kowane-model, masu aiki da waɗanda suka ƙare-har-sake-saitawa |
| `tokenmaxxing status --force` | Ƙari da haka a ping kowane asusu (ƙaramar buƙatar haiku ɗaya kowanne) don duk agogon zaman awa 5 su fara yanzu, sannan a ɗauki sabon samfur |
| `tokenmaxxing watch [seconds]` | Matsayi kai tsaye, ana sake nunawa kowace daƙiƙa N (tsohuwa 120, mafi ƙasa 30; ba ya taɓa ping) |
| `tokenmaxxing config [get\|set\|unset\|tidy]` | Tsarin da ke aiki tare da tushe; gyara maɓallan da aka raba da aya; `tidy` yana yanke maɓallan da ba a sani ba |
| `tokenmaxxing check` | Auna shawarar musanya sau ɗaya (wannan shi ne abin da agogon lokaci-lokaci ke gudanarwa) |
| `tokenmaxxing serve [setup\|link\|unlink\|links]` | Daemon na gadar Slack (Socket Mode, babu URL na jama'a) |
| `tokenmaxxing doctor` | Tabbatar da cewa mai kula da shigarwar saituna sun tsira |
| `tokenmaxxing rename [--codex] <sel> <label>` | Sake yiwa asusun da ke cikin tafki lakabi |
| `tokenmaxxing rm <sel>` | Cire asusu daga tafki |
| `tokenmaxxing uninstall` | Cire mai kula da shigarwar saituna (ana kiyaye asusu da tantancewa) |
