title: How switching dey decide
description: "Engagement, screening bars, pace pressure, and the greedy vs hard paths"
---

The decision get two layers: one engagement gate, and then one greedy or hard path.

## Engagement

Below `policy.greedySessionFloor` (default 80, for percent of the 5-hour session window), the decision no dey run at all - a fresh session dey ride im account. Once the active session window don use 80%, or any bar don cross, the decision dey engage.

## Screening bars

The thresholds na screening bars, no be the trigger for panic:

- `thresholds.session`: na ladder, default `[90]`, na only one rung. Every evaluation dey resolve one active rung: na the lowest rung wia some pooled account, including the current one, still fit work. With ladder wey get plenty rungs like `[50, 80, 95]`, as long as any account still dey below 50, the bar na 50, so a seat wey cross 50 go hand over give dat account. Once every account don pass 50, the bar dey climb go 80, and once everywhere don pass 80 e dey climb go 95. The top rung na the terminal bar; after am, na the wall (below) go take over. One 5-hour reset cheap make you sit am out, so a pool wey get plenty rungs dey drain level by level and no account dey run far ahead of im siblings.
- `thresholds.weekly`: 98. Dis one bar dey cover both the 7-day aggregate and every per-model weekly cap. Weekly quota na use-am-or-lose-am, so e dey drain close to the wall.

The two of dem dey deliberately below 100: the headroom na the budget to reach one clean turn boundary (plus up to one turn of adoption lag for macOS) before the account go actually hit im limit. The same bars dey screen swap candidates: an account wey pass any of im bars no be valid target. Trigger and screening dey always use the same effective bars (the active rung and the weekly bar, each one minus `policy.projectionMargin`), because one screen wey laxer pass the trigger go land swaps for accounts wey the trigger go immediately re-flag, and e go ping-pong the pool. One rung dey count only accounts wey still fit work for am: one dead grant, one gated per-model cap wey don burn, or one server-pinned enforcement no dey hold the bar down, and dem dey re-read the rung from the pool for every retry inside one evaluation. Codex dey read only the top rung, because restart na im own switch.

## The target: pace pressure

Among usable accounts, the target na the one wey dey furthest behind im own weekly pace: highest **pace pressure**, wey dem define as remaining weekly percent divide by time until dat account im weekly reset. The account with the most quota wey dey about to lose na im go win. Ties dey break to soonest weekly expiry, then lowest 7-day usage.

Everything dey run off cached windows wey dem store as absolute UTC epochs, so even one stale snapshot still dey resolve correctly: a weekly reset wey don pass dey extrapolate forward for exact 7-day steps, and any window wey pass im cached reset dey count as freshly empty (never a reason to switch, never a reason to keep an account benched).

## Greedy vs hard path

- **Greedy path** (engaged, but under every bar): rank every account, current one included. If the current account don already win or tie, do nothing. Otherwise swap go the strictly better account. E no dey wait at all and e no dey pre-park at all - a usable current account no be something wey you go trade for a wait. Because the current account dey ranked too, both the automatic path and bare `tokenmaxxing switch` dey idempotent: to evaluate periodically dey converge for the right account and then e dey stay put.

  The automatic greedy path dey hold the incumbent with hysteresis. Pace pressure na continuous value, so two accounts wey near-tie almost never dey tie exactly, and as you dey work the live account e dey keep nudging one parked account small ahead - na so one pool take dey ping-pong the seat every few minutes before, and every swap dey bust the per-organization prompt cache and dey pay full uncached re-read for the next turn. `policy.greedySwapMargin` (default 0.15) na the fix: one parked account must beat the current one im pace pressure by more than dis fraction before the greedy path go swap. Handoff go then only happen once the incumbent don drain enough make e genuinely dey behind, no be on lead wey small reach rounding level. The margin no dey ever soften one bar wey don cross, the hard path, or the wall - na only lateral swaps among accounts wey all dey under the bars. Bare `tokenmaxxing switch` dey set the margin to zero, so manual switch still dey converge on the strictly best account.
- **Hard path** (a bar cross): swap comot go the best usable candidate. If one candidate im refresh token turn out to dey dead, dem go flag am needs-reauth and dem go try the next candidate. If nothing dey usable, the wall squeeze (below) go run, and na only when every usable account don hit im wall the depleted-wait logic go run; only a caller wey fit actually pause the session (the supervised Stop hook) dey allowed to pre-park go one still-blocked but soonest-recovering account.

## Di wall

When the hard path no find any usable candidate - every account don pass im screening bars - the automatic decision no dey park sharp-sharp. E dey first re-evaluate against the **wall bars** (`hardThresholds`, default 100, na each account im real server limit): the session dey hold im seat and dey squeeze as e still dey under the wall, else e go swap go the best account wey still dey under wall (same pace-pressure ranking), and e go only park once every usable account don truly hit im wall. An account wey im refresh token don die, dem go set am aside for reauth, no be say dem go wait for am. Dem dey measure recovery against the wall, so an account wey im 5-hour window reset fit squeeze again even while im weekly window still dey sit above the screening bar.

Dis one dey keep the last few percent of each account im quota make e no waste. The wall reading dey come from whatever usage source wey the decision don already dey use - the statusline tee on one supervised interactive session, or one `/usage` probe on one headless run - the same figures wey Claude im `/rate-limit-options` dey show, so an account wey genuinely max out, dem go detect am and skip am. One single-turn overshoot, dem go catch am the next time wey the decision run - the periodic check, or the next spawn or Stop boundary on one headless run.

`hardThresholds` dey subtract `policy.projectionMargin` the same way the screening bars dey do, so if you set am equal to `thresholds` e go disable the wall squeeze. Na Claude-only path be dis: bare `tokenmaxxing switch` dey keep im cache-only park, and Codex dey ignore `hardThresholds` completely - a running Codex no fit adopt a swapped credential mid-session, so e dey stay on im current account reach the real server limit (a custom wall wey dey below 100 no dey apply to Codex).

## Per-model caps

`policy.switchModels` (default `["fable"]`) dey name the model families wey dia per-model weekly cap fit trigger a switch. Matching dey by family token, never by exact display string: model display names dey drift ("Fable" become "Fable 5"), and one exact-string gate one time silently disable per-model switching entirely. Two related safety rules:

- One unknown active model dey gate every configured family. Right after a swap clear snapshots, a headless machine fit run model-blind; to treat unknown as unconstrained one time let one account sit for im Fable cap for hours without anybody notice am.
- Candidate screening dey count gated per-model caps too, so a pool wey every account don burn im Fable cap no dey round-robin.

## Cooldown

One 45-second post-swap cooldown dey gate the automatic path. Dem dey suppress the statusline tee for the same 45 seconds after a swap (adoption dey take up to 30 seconds for macOS), so an earlier evaluation for run for data wey the swap imself don invalidate - na dat exact scenario one time produce one A-to-B-and-back respawn loop. Manual `tokenmaxxing switch` no dey affected by the cooldown.

## Enforced limits

The cached figures fit lag behind wetin dey happen for ground: the live account im per-model cap dey come only from one `/usage` probe of the busy live token, wey plenty times no dey print anything when session load heavy. When the server actually refuse one turn, Claude Code dey end am for one API error and e dey fire im `StopFailure` hook instead of `Stop`. tokenmaxxing dey install dat hook with one `rate_limit` matcher, and e dey read the transcript im structured error row (na the same row wey Claude Code dey mark `isApiErrorMessage`): one `quotaLimits` block dey name the window (`five_hour`, `seven_day`, or one per-model type) with the server im reset, and the Fable cap failure, wey no carry any `quotaLimits`, dem dey recognize am from the client im own error rendering plus the 429 body. Dem no dey ever read model prose.

The hook dey stamp the enforced window as 100 for dat account and e dey pin one server-dated session or weekly limit on the account till dat reset, so one account wey never get any usage sample no fit get picked straight back. E dey hand the enforcement give the decision as one fact wey strong pass any cached window (e dey bypass the post-swap cooldown, e dey gate candidates by the family wey trip, and e no dey ever hold the seat), and the hard path dey swap comot. For one supervised session wey im main turn fail, e dey also drop one respawn marker: the supervisor dey relaunch `--resume` for the fresh account and e dey submit one continuation prompt so the work wey break go pick up again. One failure inside one subagent dey swap but e no dey ever respawn (the parent turn still dey run), and one unsupervised session dey get the swap and e dey adopt am for im next request.

One failure dey count only when e clear say na for the post-swap credential e happen: one relaunch after the swap, or one request wey fail wey land after the swap plus the cooldown. One rate-limit refusal dey arrive for request start, so na the error row im own time be the anchor, no matter how long the turn run. Na dat one dey make one retrigger sequence fit cross plenty burnt accounts without one request wey still dey hold old token go stamp the fresh seat.

## Check cadence

The periodic timer dey run `tokenmaxxing check --if-due` once per tick (`policy.checkIntervalMs`, 60 seconds by default), and every evaluation dey schedule the next one, wey dem dey count from the tick im start, from the live account im headroom go im nearest bar (the active session rung, the weekly aggregate, and every gated per-model cap): 40 points or more of headroom dey sleep five ticks, 20 dey sleep three, 8 dey sleep two, and anything below dat dey evaluate every tick. The ladder dey also cap dat sleep one band for every rung wey dem climb: for the first rung na up to five ticks, for the second na up to three, for the third na up to two, and any rung after dat na every tick, so a pool wey don climb one level dey get poll more often pass one wey still dey for im first rung. One tick wey land within half tick of the due time dey count as due, so one slow evaluation no fit push one band comot by one whole tick. Bare `tokenmaxxing check` dey ignore the schedule and e dey evaluate sharp-sharp. One tee wey old pass the poll TTL, or no tee at all for the live org, dey keep the three-tick cadence, and one gated family wey no get cap row no dey ever sleep pass two ticks, so one window wey dem never measure no fit ever buy the slow lane. One depleted wait dey sleep toward im reset, one tick wey dey inside the post-swap cooldown dey sleep exactly reach im end, and one cap row wey old pass the poll TTL dey count as unmeasured. One swap dey clear the schedule so dem go evaluate the fresh seat once the cooldown lift. The slow lane dey safe only because the StopFailure hook dey catch enforcement the moment e happen.
