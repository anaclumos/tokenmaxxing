---
title: Tsarin babban shirin yanayi
description: "Kowane fayil da babban shiri ƙarƙashin ~/.config/tokenmaxxing"
---

Komai yana zaune ƙarƙashin `~/.config/tokenmaxxing/` (ana iya sauya shi da `TOKENMAXXING_HOME`; kowace hanya da ake iya gani daga waje tana da sauyawar env don dukkan kayan aikin ya iya gudana a keɓe cikin gwaje-gwaje).

| Hanya | Abin da yake riƙe |
|---|---|
| `config.json` | Saitunanka (bai cika ba; tsohuwar ƙima tana aiki ga kowane fili) |
| `accounts.json` | Fihirisar da ba ta sirri ba ta asusun Claude da ke cikin tafki: lakabai, id, tagogin amfani da aka ajiye. Ba tokens ba |
| `usage.json` | Samfurin tagar tarin na tee na statusline; mtime nasa shi ne bugun zuciyar rayuwar abincin |
| `model-usage.json` | Iyakokin mako na kowane-model da aka ajiye da TTL daga `/usage` |
| `lastswap.json` | Hatimin lokaci na epoch na musanya ta ƙarshe; yana tuƙa sanyaya na daƙiƙa 45 |
| `lock` | Fayil ɗin `flock` da ke jera musanya |
| `tokenmaxxing.log` | Log na al'amura na ƙari-kawai |
| `bin/` | Shims na PATH: `claude`, `codex`, da wuraren shiga na `tokenmaxxing` / `xx` |
| `respawn/<session-id>` | Alamomin sake-farawa na jira-ƙarewa da mai kula ke kallo |
| `creds/` | Linux kawai: fayilolin tantancewa da aka ajiye, 0600 cikin babban shirin 0700 (macOS yana ajiye tantancewa a matsayin abubuwan keychain na kowane asusu maimakon haka) |
| `onboard/`, `sample/` | Gidajen saitin da aka ware na jefarwa don shiga na `add`/`auth` da probes na `/usage` na asusun da aka ajiye; gidajen onboard da ke riƙe tantancewa bayyananne ana share su bayan tattarawa |
| `slack.json` | Tokens na `xx serve` da haɗin tashoshi (0600) |
| `slack-threads/` | Bayanan zama na kowane zare don `xx serve` |
| `codex-*` | Tafkin Codex mai gudana a layi ɗaya: `codex-accounts.json`, `codex-lastswap.json`, `codex-lock`, `codex-creds/`, `codex-onboard/`, `codex-respawn/`, da `codex-live/` (fayil ɗin kasancewa guda da aka tabbatar da PID ga kowane zaman codex da ake kula da shi mai gudana, don asusu mai aiki ba ya taɓa zama abin nufi na musanya) |

A wajen babban shirinsa, tokenmaxxing yana taɓa ainihin waɗannan: `~/.claude.json` (musanyar `oauthAccount`), `settings.json` na Claude Code (shigarwar hook da statusline), ma'ajin tantancewar da ke aiki (abin keychain na `Claude Code-credentials` a kan macOS, `.credentials.json` a kan Linux), `~/.claude.lock` (makullin sabuntawa na claude, da ake ɗauka yayin musanya), `auth.json` da `hooks.json` na codex, da na'urar dubawa-ta-lokaci-lokaci a cikin launchd ko systemd.
