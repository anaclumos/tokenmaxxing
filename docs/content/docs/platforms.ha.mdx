---
title: "Bayanan dandali: macOS da Linux"
description: "Yadda macOS da Linux suka bambanta a ajiya, amincewa, da agogo"
---

| | macOS | Linux |
|---|---|---|
| Tantancewar da ke aiki | Abin generic-password na keychain na shiga | Fayil ɗin `.credentials.json` na 0600 |
| Tantancewar da aka ajiye | Abubuwan keychain na kowane asusu | Fayilolin 0600 cikin babban shirin 0700 |
| Amincewa bayan musanya | Cikin kusan daƙiƙa 30 (ajiyar karatun keychain) | Buƙata ta gaba (dubawar mtime na fayil) |
| Dubawa na lokaci-lokaci | launchd agent, tick na ƙasa na 60s, mai daidaita kansa 60s zuwa 300s | systemd user timer, tick na ƙasa na 60s, mai daidaita kansa 60s zuwa 300s |
| Ma'ajin shiga-a-ware | Sabis na keychain mai suna-hash ga kowane babban shirin saitin | Fayil a cikin babban shirin saitin da aka ware |

Bayanai guda biyu na musamman ga Linux da suka cancanci sani: claude yana warware babban shirin tantancewarsa daga `CLAUDE_SECURESTORAGE_CONFIG_DIR` da farko (sannan `CLAUDE_CONFIG_DIR`, sannan `~/.claude`), kuma tokenmaxxing yana kwaikwayon wannan warwarewa daidai don kada su rabu. Kuma gine-ginen OS guda biyu na sigar claude iri ɗaya suna tsara agogon sake saiti na `/usage` daban-daban (ɗaya yana amfani da haɗin " at ", ɗayan waƙafi); mai fassarar tokenmaxxing yana karɓar waɗannan sifofi biyu daidai kuma yana rubutawa, maimakon hasashe, a kan kowane na uku.

A kan macOS, iyaka guda ta gaskiya: `init` da `add` suna taɓa keychain ta hulɗa da gangan, don farkon shiga na `security` kada ya zama sanyi cikin hook mara kai.
