---
title: Saitawa
description: "Tsarin config.json, ƙofofi, da maɓallan manufa"
---

Saituna JSON ne a `~/.config/tokenmaxxing/config.json`. Kowane fili na zaɓi ne; tsohuwar ƙima tana aiki ga kowane fili.

```json
{
  "thresholds": { "session": [50, 80, 95], "weekly": 98 },
  "hardThresholds": { "session": 100, "weekly": 100 },
  "claudeBin": "/path/to/real/claude",
  "codexBin": "/path/to/real/codex",
  "policy": {
    "projectionMargin": 0,
    "greedySessionFloor": 50,
    "switchModels": ["fable"],
    "usagePollTtlMs": 90000,
    "maxWaitMs": 3600000
  }
}
```

- `thresholds.session`: tsanin awa 5, jeri mai hawa na matakan tantancewa. Kowace auna tana warware mataki guda mai aiki, wato mataki mafi ƙasa wanda wani asusu na tafkin har yanzu yake ƙarƙashinsa, kuma wannan mataki shi ne duka abin jawowa da kuma tantance 'yan takara. Codex yana karanta mataki mafi sama kaɗai. Duba [Yadda musanya ke yanke shawara](/ha/switching).
- `thresholds.weekly`: sandar tantancewa ta mako, tana rufe tarin kwanaki 7 da kowane iyakar mako na kowane-model.
- `hardThresholds.session` / `hardThresholds.weekly`: sandunan bango (tsohuwa 100, ainihin iyakar sabar na kowane asusu). `hardThresholds.session` dole ne ya kasance daidai da mataki mafi sama na zama ko sama da shi. Idan kowane asusu ya wuce sandar tantancewarsa, shawarar atomatik ta Claude tana matsa kowanne har zuwa bangonsa kafin ajiyewa - duba [Bango](/ha/switching). Saita shi daidai da mataki mafi sama da sandar mako don kashewa; Codex yana yin watsi da wannan saiti.
- `claudeBin` / `codexBin`: yana daure zuwa AINIHIN binaries da masu kula ke ƙaddamarwa (an rubuta su ta `init` / `init --codex`; ganewar-matsalar na kariyar sake-kira ta gaya maka ka gyara `claudeBin` a nan idan ya daina nunawa zuwa ainihin binary na Claude).
- `policy.projectionMargin`: ana rage shi daga kowane matakin zama, sandar mako, da sandunan bango don samar da sandunan da ke aiki.
- `policy.greedySessionFloor`: kason da aka yi amfani-zama a wanda haɗuwar greedy ke shiga.
- `policy.switchModels`: iyalan model waɗanda iyakar mako na kowane-model ɗinsu ke toshe musanya (ana mai da shigarwa ƙananan haruffa lokacin lodi).
- `policy.usagePollTtlMs`: tsawon lokacin da dubawar kowane-model na `/usage` ke ci gaba da sabo.
- `policy.maxWaitMs`: iyakar jiran atomatik na tafki da ya ƙare.

`tokenmaxxing config` yana nuna saitunan da ke aiki tare da tushe; `config set`/`unset` suna gyara maɓallan da aka raba da aya; `config tidy` yana yanke maɓallan da ba a sani ba.
