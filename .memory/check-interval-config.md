User request 2026-09-06: "Make xx switch timer configurable." Before this the tick was a hard-coded `CHECK_INTERVAL_S = 60` in `install.ts` while the `init` success line still said "every 3m" (stale since the 180 to 60 change in 1.9.0), and the self-paced bands were absolute milliseconds (60s, 120s, 180s, 300s).

Landing as 1.14.0 on branch `feat/check-interval-config`; the shipped record goes here only after the release and the npm read succeed. The change: `policy.checkIntervalMs` (integer, minimum 10000, default 60000) is the one cadence knob. The minimum is launchd's default `ThrottleInterval`: the launchd.plist man page says jobs are not spawned more than once every 10 seconds, so a shorter `StartInterval` is silently throttled (cubic finding on PR #62). `installSupervisor` reads it, rounds up to whole seconds, writes it as launchd `StartInterval` and systemd `OnUnitActiveSec`, and returns it as `checkIntervalS` for the init line. `checkDelayMs` derives every band from the tick: one tick near a bar, two at 8 points of headroom, three at 20, five at 40 (`MAX_CHECK_DELAY_TICKS`), the per-rung ceilings are `STAGE_CEILING_TICKS = [5, 3, 2]`, the no-tee fallback is three ticks, and a gated family without a cap row is capped at two. `loadNextCheckDueAt` takes the config so its far-future guard is the larger of five ticks and the 45s post-swap cooldown (`POST_SWAP_COOLDOWN_MS` moved to `state.ts` for that), and the `--if-due` slack is half a tick. A depleted wait and the cooldown sleep to their own deadlines, not to a tick multiple. The systemd `AccuracySec` is a twelfth of the tick with a one-second floor, in the installer and both Nix timer modules.

**Why:** a knob that scaled only the OS tick would leave the schedule sleeping past it or under it; a knob that scaled only the schedule could never go below the installed tick. Scaling both from one value keeps the documented shape (one to five ticks) at any tick.

**How to apply:** `config set` and `config unset` on this key print a note to re-run `tokenmaxxing init`, because the timer unit is only rewritten there and `config` must stay inside the state root. A longer tick takes effect on the next check without re-init (the installed tick still fires and `--if-due` skips until due); a shorter tick needs the re-init. The Nix `checkTimer.intervalSeconds` option is the declarative equivalent and has to be kept equal by hand. Hermetic proof method in [[hermetic-ladder-verification]]: with the tick at 30000, a seat at 85 that wins on pace holds with `next in 30s`, a seat at 79 holds unengaged with `next in 60s`, and no tee gives `next in 90s`.
